Work

    about

    We design objects that sit quietly in everyday life — and work hard the moment you reach for them.

    TEUM is an industrial design studio in Seongsu, Seoul. We work with manufacturers and start-ups on mobility, robotics, wearables and the objects of the home — from the first field visit to the first production run.

    Our process is physical. Foam, paper and printed parts sit on every desk, and nothing is approved until it has been held, carried and lived with for a week.
